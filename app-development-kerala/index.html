Questions

Why Flutter instead of separate Android and iOS apps?

Flutter lets one codebase run on both platforms, so you pay for one build and get updates on both at the same time.

Can you build the backend and admin panel too?

Yes. I build the server, database and admin panel, and host it on a VPS with SSL.

Will you publish the app to the Play Store and App Store?

I prepare the builds and store listings and guide you through publishing under your own developer accounts.

Can I start with a smaller first version?

Yes. Launching a focused first version and adding features later is often the smartest way to start.

Who owns the app and the code?

You do. The app is published under your accounts and you get the source code.
