Questions

Is it real footage?

No. The scenes are generated with AI video tools from a written script and storyboard, then edited together with your branding. It is clearly a creative video, not a recording of your premises.

Can you include my products, logo or shop?

Your logo, text and brand colours are added in the edit. Your own product photos can also be used as a starting point for scenes.

Can the video have Malayalam text?

Yes. On-screen text and captions can be in Malayalam, English or both.

Which formats will I get?

Vertical 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, square 1:1 for feeds and wide 16:9 for YouTube or your website, as needed.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?

Yes, you can use them across your social media and ads.
